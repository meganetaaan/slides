Some robots depend on a company's servers for conversation. Those services need people and money to keep running, and sometimes they have to end. I saw sad news about robots that could no longer talk, even though they were still in people's homes. I wondered whether an open community could help people keep their robots going. That idea became Stack-chan: a palm-sized, open-source robot with two motors and a simple face. Its slogan is “A communication robot in your hands.” That means both a robot small enough to hold, and the freedom to change it and help shape its future.
